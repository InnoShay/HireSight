## 🎨 DESIGN GUIDELINES

### Colors
- **Background**: Dark navy (#0a0a1a) or deep purple (#1a1a2e)
- **Primary text**: White (#ffffff)
- **Accent 1**: Electric blue (#00d4ff)
- **Accent 2**: Purple (#8b5cf6)
- **Success**: Green (#10b981)
- **Warning**: Orange (#f59e0b)

### Typography
- **Headlines**: Bold, large (48-72pt)
- **Body**: Regular, readable (24-32pt)
- **Font**: Inter, Outfit, or system sans-serif

### Visual Style
- Minimal text (max 6 bullet points per slide)
- Icons over text where possible
- Consistent spacing and alignment
- Subtle gradients and glow effects
- No clipart—use clean icons or screenshots
