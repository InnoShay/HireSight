## 🧠 THE TWO AI ENGINES

### Engine 1: TensorFlow.js + Universal Sentence Encoder
- **What**: Converts text to 512-dimensional vectors
- **Why**: Understands meaning, not just keywords
- **Speed**: Instant (milliseconds)

### Engine 2: Google Gemini 2.5 Flash
- **What**: Deep analysis and insights generation
- **Why**: Extracts skills, validates experience, gives recommendations
- **Speed**: 2-3 seconds

### Why Both?
> "TensorFlow gives us **speed and objectivity**. Gemini gives us **depth and explanations**. Together, they're unbeatable."

## 🛡️ HANDLING TOUGH QUESTIONS

### "What about AI bias?"
> "Our prompts focus on objective skills and experience, not demographics. The semantic matching is purely content-based. We plan to add a formal bias audit layer."

### "How do you handle rate limits?"
> "We built a round-robin API key orchestrator. 7 keys rotating = 420 requests/minute. Zero failures."

### "Is this production-ready?"
> "Yes! We're deployed on Vercel, tested with 100+ resumes, and the live demo is at hiresight-delta.vercel.app"
