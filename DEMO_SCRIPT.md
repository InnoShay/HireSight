## 🎤 VOICE TIPS

- **Pace**: Speak clearly and at a moderate pace (~150 words/minute)
- **Enthusiasm**: Sound excited about the features—you built something amazing!
- **Pauses**: Pause briefly when transitioning between sections
- **Emphasis**: Stress key phrases like "5 seconds", "industry-first", "explainable AI"
- **Natural**: It's okay to deviate slightly from the script if it feels more natural

---

## 🖥️ SCREEN RECORDING TIPS

1. **Resolution**: Record at 1920x1080 or higher
2. **Cursor**: Use a cursor highlighter if possible
3. **Zoom**: Zoom into important areas when explaining
4. **Smooth scrolling**: Scroll slowly so viewers can follow
5. **Wait for loading**: Pause when things are loading—don't cut away
